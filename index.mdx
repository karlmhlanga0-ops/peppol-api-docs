Welcome to the Octothorp Peppol API. We provide the serverless infrastructure that allows African and global e-commerce platforms to automatically clear mandatory government tax invoices in real-time.

No XML cryptography, no AS4 servers, no corporate sales calls. Just one JSON endpoint.
